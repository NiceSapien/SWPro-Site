Release vs. Nightly

Release Builds Recommended

Tested and chosen by the maintainers for public use. Best choice for reliable app development without unexpected breakage.

Nightly Builds Experimental

Compiled automatically from the latest developer commits. Includes bleeding-edge changes, but may be unstable and is not recommended for daily projects.
